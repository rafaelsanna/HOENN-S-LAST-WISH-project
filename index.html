The Story

A parallel universe where Rayquaza failed to stop Groudon and Kyogre, which caused a natural collapse in Hoenn.

After long years of natural destruction, the entire Hoenn region was practically devastated, the era of trainers / pokeballs ended, the pokémons became wild animals, and the nomadic people trying to survive.

After years the plot is the millennium comet (Jirachi) and Celebi (time traveler), giving the player an opportunity to go back in time and prevent this disaster from happening.

With the return in time, some things from Hoenn destroyed for many years affect the "normal" Hoenn of the past, generating new forms of pokémon, new characters, new villains, gym leaders, and more.

Play as Zinnia or Zenno, uncovering the past events that led to Hoenn's destruction, and then travel back in time to experience a parallel reality where you can avert the tragedy.

Expect captivating, individually crafted characters with depth and personality, including a secondary rival: Luka, who plays an active role in the story, and villains driven by genuine, greed-fueled commercial ambitions. With other supporting characters also boasting greater depth, do not expect the same Hoenn story you already know; things have changed completely.

Modern, fully customizable quality-of-life features to enhance the experience; clean, unique menus and UIs; increased difficulty with options for both casual and competitive players; built-in Nuzlocke and randomizer modes; plus various other features seen in top-tier projects, alongside unique ones we created specifically for this game.
