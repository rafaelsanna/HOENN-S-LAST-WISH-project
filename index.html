The Story

Picture Hoenn already falling apart once. Rayquaza couldn't stop Groudon and Kyogre in time, and by the time the dust settled, there wasn't much of a region left.

No more Trainers, no more Poké Balls, just scattered survivors trying to get through the day in a Hoenn that's been taken back by wild, untamed Pokémon. Rough place to grow up in.

Then the Millennium Comet shows up, and with it come Jirachi and Celebi offering something nobody saw coming: a chance to go back and stop all of this before it ever happens.

So you get a second shot as a normal Trainer in a Hoenn that looks familiar... mostly. Turns out fixing the timeline doesn't mean it holds together perfectly. Weird stuff keeps slipping through the cracks:

  • Pokémon forms nobody's ever seen before
  • Familiar faces acting a little off
  • Old rivalries between factions that suddenly feel a lot more serious
  • Gym Leaders dealing with problems way above their pay grade

This isn't just another badge run. You're the only one who remembers what almost happened — and that's starting to feel like a problem.

Every battle and every choice pulls you a little deeper into what's really going on. Hoenn got a second wish. Whether it deserves one is up to you.
