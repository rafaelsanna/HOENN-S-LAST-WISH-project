# Hoenn's Last Wish — Story Walkthrough

> **Full-story spoilers:** This guide follows the main story from the opening dream through the first defeat of Champion Stella. It highlights required navigation, Gyms, story battles, scripted scenes, and notable rewards. Ordinary pickups and routine Trainer battles are omitted.

Names in this guide follow the player-facing dialogue. Your rival is **Zinnia** when playing as the male protagonist and **Zenno** when playing as the female protagonist; “the rival” means either character.

## 1. The Dream Realm and the optional prologue

The game opens in a dream with **Jirachi** and **Celebi**. Jirachi recounts the ancient clash between **Groudon**, titan of the land, and **Kyogre**, deity of the sea. Their struggle brought eruptions, floods, and the destruction of cities. The legends also tell of time travel, wishes, ancient shrines, Jirachi's awakening, and the Millennium Comet. Jirachi says a chosen person may be able to alter the past and save the future.

When asked whether to play the prologue:

- Choose **Yes** for the full ruined-future sequence below. This is strongly recommended for the complete story and its exclusive early reward.
- Choose **No** to wake in Littleroot and continue at section 2.

### Ruined Mossdeep

You awaken in a devastated future version of Mossdeep. Speak with your grandparents. Hoenn is dying, Poké Balls no longer exist, and you have no Pokémon with which to defend yourself. They give you the **Shiny Charm**.

Explore until the hostile Mightyena attacks. You cannot win this encounter; your rival gets you back to safety. Your grandparents suggest finding **Luka**, whose Pidgeot can help. Speak to Luka after the attack. He and Pidgeot move ahead and hold the dangerous wild Pokémon back.

Collect the three visible Time Gears:

- **Time Gear of the Past:** shining in the eastern grass.
- **Time Gear of the Present:** glowing among rocks on the far eastern side.
- **Time Gear of the Future:** sparkling in the water to the west.

Find the trapped Celebi and return after collecting all three gears. Celebi restores enough of the temporal flow to calm the wild Pokémon, joins you as a follower, and calls a Wailord that creates a path across the water.

Cross the new path and approach the sleeping Jirachi near the northern rocks. Jirachi will not complete the ritual unless Celebi has been freed and all three Time Gears have been found. Speak to Jirachi to see the Millennium Comet appear. Jirachi restores the timeline and sends you out of the dream.

There are also short survivor-rescue scenes around the ruined map. They add atmosphere but are not required for the Time Gear objective.

## 2. Littleroot, Acacia, and the first rival battle

After waking in Littleroot, visit the neighboring house and meet your rival. Then head north through Oldale toward Route 101.

On Route 101, **Professor Acacia** is being chased by a Sentret. Choose a starter from the bag and defeat it. Acacia takes you to the lab, recognizes your potential, and lets you keep the Pokémon.

Travel to Route 103 and battle your rival. The rival has also noticed the Millennium Comet and says Acacia has been watching your progress. After winning, return to Acacia's lab. You receive the **Pokédex** and **Town Map**. Acacia explains that the comet may have changed reality and asks you to travel Hoenn and investigate. She also directs you toward her partner, **Calendula**, the Petalburg Gym Leader.

## 3. Petalburg, Luka, and the road to Rustboro

At Petalburg Gym, Calendula introduces you to **Luka**, an energetic young Trainer studying the comet. Luka battles you on Route 102; the story continues whether you win or lose. Back at the Gym, Calendula gives you an **Eevee Egg** and an **Everstone**, but will not accept your Gym challenge until you have four Badges.

Continue through Route 104 and Petalburg Woods. Two Team Aqua grunts attack a Devon employee and try to steal the Devon Goods. Defeat both grunts. The employee gives you an **Ability Patch** for helping, then you can continue to Rustboro.

### Gym 1 — Terra

Challenge **Terra** at Rustboro Gym. Victory earns the **Stone Badge** and **TM Rock Tomb**.

### Devon Goods and Mr. Stone

After the Gym, a thief runs toward Route 116. Follow through Rusturf Tunnel, defeat the Team Aqua grunt, recover the **Devon Goods**, and rescue Mr. Briney's **Nami**.

On returning the goods in Rustboro, the Devon employee gives you an **Ability Capsule** and escorts you to **Mr. Stone**. Mr. Stone asks you to deliver a **Letter** to his granddaughter Stella in Dewford and the Devon Goods to Captain Stern in Slateport. He also gives you the **DexNav**.

Before leaving Rustboro, speak to your rival. The rival battle can initially be declined, but the route remains blocked until you settle it. Win to receive the **Exp. Share** and learn that Mr. Briney can sail you from Route 104.

Return to Mr. Briney's cottage and sail to Dewford. Your rival accompanies the voyage and says goodbye after landing.

## 4. Dewford, Aurora, and Stella

Enter Granite Cave. Near the entrance, **Aurora** claims the comet blessed her with extraordinary power and challenges you to test the unusual energy she senses in you. Defeat her to receive **HM Flash**. Aurora warns that a great crisis is approaching, then teleports away.

### Gym 2 — Renton

Defeat **Renton** at Dewford Gym for the **Knuckle Badge** and **TM Power-Up Punch**. Speak to him again after the victory to receive the **Radio**.

Now use Flash and continue through Granite Cave. Defeat the Team Magma members inside and find **Stella** in the deepest room. Give her Mr. Stone's Letter. She gives you **TM Steel Wing** and shows you an ancient painting of the land and sea titans. Stella suspects both the comet and Team Magma may be connected to the old legend.

If you return to Mr. Stone after delivering Stella's Letter, he rewards you with a **Shiny Stone**.

## 5. Slateport, Team Aqua, and Mauville

Ask Mr. Briney to sail to Slateport. Take the Devon Goods to Captain Stern at the Oceanic Museum. Defeat the Team Aqua members who confront Stern; **Alejandro** states Team Aqua's intention to expand the sea before withdrawing.

Continue north along Route 110. Your rival challenges you again and gives you the **Dowsing Machine** after the battle.

In Mauville, Luka is arguing with his uncle outside the Gym. Luka has focused heavily on researching the comet and wants to prove he is ready for the Gym. Accept his challenge and defeat him; he recognizes that he needs more training and returns toward Verdanturf.

Pick up **HM Rock Smash** from the house in southeast Mauville, then challenge the Gym.

### Gym 3 — Den

Defeat **Den** for the **Dynamo Badge** and **TM Volt Switch**.

West of Mauville, visit Verdanturf and clear the breakable rocks in Rusturf Tunnel. Reuniting the couple there earns **HM Strength**.

## 6. Meteor Falls, Mt. Chimney, and Lavaridge

Travel north through Route 111, Fiery Path, Route 113, and Fallarbor to Meteor Falls. Team Magma steals a Meteorite fragment that fell with the Millennium Comet. They believe it “remembers” a lost version of the world and intend to use it at Mt. Chimney. Team Aqua arrives too late and pursues Magma.

After the teams leave, speak to **Calendula**. The grunts had tried to take her research. She explains that the cavern and meteorite contain energy matching the Millennium Comet, then leaves to compare her notes with the instruments at Mossdeep Space Center.

Return toward Mt. Chimney and ride the cable car up. Fight through Team Magma, defeat administrator **Netsu**, and then defeat leader **Magnus** at the meteorite machine. Magnus is trying to use the Meteorite's power to reshape Hoenn through the volcano. Remove the **Meteorite** from the machine after the battle.

Descend Jagged Pass to Lavaridge.

### Gym 4 — Amaterasu

Defeat **Amaterasu** for the **Heat Badge** and **TM Fiery Dance**. Outside, your rival gives you the **Go-Goggles**, allowing you to cross Route 111's desert.

## 7. Phoenix Town, Aurora Grove, and Nightmare Petalburg

Return to Petalburg. An earthquake and unnatural storm have struck, Calendula is missing, and the Gym is closed. Follow the clue that Calendula went to the desert after receiving a letter. Cross Route 111's desert and continue to Phoenix Town.

There you find Calendula, Luka, and your rival waiting near the cave. **Aurora** appears and reveals that she sent the letter. She believes the Millennium Comet is a savior trying to reverse the events that caused the world to become ruined, and that only someone with a special “gift” can repair the timeline. She rejects Luka and the rival as candidates, then challenges you.

Defeat Aurora. She confirms that you carry the gift and asks you to follow her. Enter Phoenix Cave and take the portal to **Aurora Grove**, a place between dreams and reality. Aurora explains that Celebi and Jirachi once repaired a broken world, but the timeline is tearing again. She grants you the ability to enter and purify nightmare rifts. She also warns that ordinary people will forget a restored timeline; only the gifted retain what happened.

Return through the cave and go back to Petalburg. Speak with Calendula. Another quake knocks you unconscious and pulls you into **Nightmare Petalburg**.

### Nightmare map: Nightmare Petalburg

This distorted Petalburg contains signs and dialogue from a false history—including a world that does not remember Luka. Explore until you reach the dark-aura Pokémon.

Defeat **Dark Suicune** in the special scripted battle. You receive a **Comet Shard**. Continue to the depleted Time Gear, then choose to turn it. Celebi appears, restores this portion of the timeline, and warns that most people will not remember what was repaired. You awaken in the real Petalburg and the Gym becomes available.

### Gym 5 — Calendula

Work through Calendula's research-themed Gym and defeat her for the **Balance Badge** and **TM Wish**. Calendula is proud of how far you have come, both as a Trainer and as someone willing to protect others.

You are then taken to Luka's house. His family thanks you for supporting him and gives you **HM Surf**.

## 8. Luka and Team Aqua's shipping operation

When you leave after the fifth Badge, Luka challenges you again in Petalburg. This battle must be won to move on. Luka warns that Team Aqua and Team Magma have been seen near the woods and that something serious is developing.

### Abandoned Ship

Use Surf to reach the Abandoned Ship on Route 108. Find the department/ship **Keys**, defeat the Aqua grunt, and confront **Inae**. After the story battle, Stella arrives. Inae retreats, and Stella warns that Team Aqua is taking control of ports and shipping routes across Hoenn.

### Cargo Ship with Luka

After clearing the Abandoned Ship, meet Luka in Slateport. He has seen Aqua members loading a cargo ship at the docks. Board the ship and defeat the Aqua member on deck. When a sailor blocks the hold, Luka arrives and has Pidgeot confuse him so the two of you can slip below deck.

Search the hold for the **Seabreeze Shipping HQ Key Card**. Luka helps you return to Slateport and goes to report the discovery to Calendula.

### Seabreeze Shipping HQ

Take the Key Card to Seabreeze Shipping. Use the elevator, fight through the Aqua-controlled offices, and reach the second floor. You overhear Alejandro and the company's president discussing an illegal transport arrangement. They flee after noticing you, leaving their accountant Juan to challenge you. Defeat Juan to break up the operation and open the route east.

## 9. Weather Institute, rival, Stella, and Fortree

Travel through Route 118—where Stella briefly checks on your progress—and continue north along Route 119. Team Aqua has occupied the Weather Institute. Clear both floors and defeat the Aqua administrator to free the researchers. A scientist offers you **Castform** as thanks; make room in your party if you want to receive it immediately.

Just beyond the Institute, your rival challenges you. Win to receive **HM Fly**.

Continue to Fortree, then go east onto Route 120. Stella demonstrates the invisible Pokémon blocking the path and gives you the **Devon Scope**. Use it to expose the Kecleon, then return to Fortree Gym.

### Gym 6 — Taka

Defeat **Taka** for the **Feather Badge** and **TM Acrobatics**.

## 10. Mt. Pyre and the Nightmare Realm

Continue through Route 121. Team Aqua moves toward Mt. Pyre after the Seabreeze operation collapses. Cross Route 122, enter Mt. Pyre, climb through the interior, and follow the exterior path to the summit.

On the exterior, Magnus reveals that Team Magma slipped down the far side while Team Aqua rushed toward the summit. At the top, fight through Team Aqua. Inae escapes with one Orb; Team Magma has already taken the other. The shrine keeper gives you the **Magma Emblem**, which opens the hidden Magma base in Jagged Pass.

The keepers then recount the legend of the chosen one beside statues of Jirachi and Celebi. A temporal rift pulls you into the **Nightmare Realm**.

### Nightmare map: Nightmare Realm

Jirachi and Celebi are being pursued by their shadow counterparts. These beings are the accumulated grief and ruin of the broken future given form, and they are trying to prevent the timeline from being repaired.

Defeat **Shadow Celebi** and **Shadow Jirachi** in the forced double battle. You cannot run, catch them, or use the Bag. After winning, speak to either Jirachi or Celebi to finish the scene. They explain that time is a river with many branches: the ruined future cannot simply be erased, but it does not have to repeat. They remain behind to anchor the boundary and return you to Mt. Pyre.

If you lose, a relic appears at the summit. Interact with it to heal your party and retry the nightmare battle.

## 11. Groudon, the submarine theft, and the seventh Gym

Take the Magma Emblem to Jagged Pass. A hidden entrance opens into Team Magma's base.

Near the entrance, you unexpectedly meet **Mr. Stone**. He claims the mine once belonged to Devon and that he is only inspecting it, then quickly leaves. This is the first direct indication that his involvement may be deeper than he admits.

Fight through the Magma Hideout and defeat the administrator near the deepest chamber. Magnus uses the wrong Orb to awaken **Groudon**. Groudon immediately escapes. Defeat Magnus; he abandons the base to pursue it.

Return to Slateport. During Captain Stern's interview about the undersea cavern, Team Aqua announces its plan and steals the Explorer 1 submarine. Travel to Lilycove and enter the now-accessible Aqua Hideout. Defeat the Aqua administrator at the submarine dock, but the submarine escapes toward the ocean depths.

Your rival is also waiting outside Lilycove's Department Store. This battle can be postponed, but it is the rival's major Lilycove encounter. After losing, the rival acknowledges your growth and flies back to Littleroot to train harder.

With the hideout cleared, travel to Mossdeep.

### Gym 7 — Soul & Luna

Defeat twin sisters **Soul and Luna** in their Fairy/Dark double battle. You receive the **Mind Badge**, **TM Dazzling Gleam**, and **TM Dark Pulse**.

### Team Magma at the Space Center

After the Gym, Team Magma attacks Mossdeep Space Center to steal rocket fuel and force another volcanic eruption. Calendula's Meteor Falls samples are already being studied here; their energy matches the irregularity recorded after the Millennium Comet.

Fight through the grunts to the second floor. Join **Stella** in a multi battle against Magnus and his administrator. Team Magma finally admits its plan has failed and withdraws.

Visit Stella's house afterward. She gives you **HM Dive** and points you toward the underwater cavern between Mossdeep and Sootopolis.

## 12. Kyogre and the Sootopolis crisis

Surf south from Mossdeep and use Dive on Route 128 to find the stolen submarine and the entrance to Seafloor Cavern. Navigate the current and boulder rooms, defeat the Aqua administrator, and reach **Inae** beside the sleeping Kyogre.

Defeat Inae. She uses the wrong Orb to awaken **Kyogre**, which escapes and creates a catastrophic downpour. Magnus arrives and explains that the uncontrolled weather will destroy far more than either team intended. Everyone heads for Sootopolis, where Groudon and Kyogre are now clashing.

Enter Sootopolis by diving. The initial cutscene shows the two titans fighting in the center of the city.

### Mr. Stone's betrayal

Approach **Mr. Stone** at the southern riverbank to trigger the central reveal. He admits that Devon financed and manipulated both Team Magma and Team Aqua. Neither team was meant to win: they were pushed into finding and misusing the Orbs so that Mr. Stone could seize both titans for himself.

Mr. Stone belittles ordinary Trainers and even his granddaughter Stella's Champion title compared with divine power. He intends to use Groudon and Kyogre to raise land, command the oceans, redirect trade routes, monopolize resources, and control governments. He boards a Devon boat with both Orbs and forces Groudon and Kyogre into their **Primal forms**, making the disaster worse. Magnus and Inae finally understand how completely Devon used them.

### Cave of Origin and Sky Pillar

Speak to Stella. She leads you to the Cave of Origin, where the ancient expert explains that a third legendary Pokémon is needed to stop the clash. The answer is **Rayquaza** at Sky Pillar.

Travel to Pacifidlog and then east to Sky Pillar. Climb to the summit and awaken Rayquaza. It flies to Sootopolis. Return there to watch Rayquaza descend and calm the Primal titans.

Afterward, speak to both Magnus and Inae. They accept the consequences of their actions and leave. As a story coda, you may return to Mt. Pyre to see them return the Orbs to the shrine keepers.

Speak to the Sootopolis guide near the Gym to receive **HM Waterfall**. The Gym is now open.

### Gym 8 — Rio

Complete the ice-floor puzzles and defeat **Rio** for the **Rain Badge** and **TM Flip Turn**. With all eight Badges and Waterfall, you can now reach Ever Grande City.

## 13. Victory Road and Luka's final pre-League battle

Surf east from Route 128 toward Ever Grande City and use Waterfall to reach the Pokémon League approach. Your rival confronts you at Ever Grande for one last required test before the League. Win, and the rival admits that they are not ready for the Elite Four yet but promises to keep training.

Enter Victory Road.

Near the entrance, **Luka** stops you for one final required story battle. He has followed the comet's call across Hoenn and refuses to keep losing without trying again. Defeat him; Luka accepts the result but promises to continue growing.

Traverse Victory Road using the field moves collected during the story, emerge at the League, and heal before entering.

## 14. Elite Four and Champion Stella

Enter the League and defeat the Elite Four in order:

1. **Tsubaki** — Dark specialist.
2. **Iara** — Ghost specialist.
3. **Stark** — Ice specialist.
4. **Daemon** — Dragon specialist.

Proceed to the Champion's room. **Stella** is both Mr. Stone's granddaughter and Hoenn's reigning Champion. Defeat her to complete the main story.

Your rival arrives just after the battle, followed by Professor Acacia. Acacia evaluates your Pokédex and congratulates you. Stella then escorts you into the Hall of Fame, where your winning team is registered and you are recognized as Hoenn's new Champion.

## Key story items at a glance

- **Shiny Charm:** from your grandparents during the optional ruined-future prologue.
- **Three Time Gears:** collected in ruined Mossdeep to free Celebi and awaken Jirachi; these are story objectives rather than normal Bag items.
- **Eevee Egg and Everstone:** from Calendula during the first Petalburg visit.
- **Exp. Share:** from the rival after the Rustboro battle.
- **DexNav:** from Mr. Stone when he assigns the Dewford and Slateport deliveries.
- **Ability Capsule:** from the Devon employee after recovering the Devon Goods.
- **Ability Patch:** from the Devon employee after saving him from the two Aqua grunts in Petalburg Woods.
- **Shiny Stone:** from Mr. Stone after Stella receives his Letter.
- **Comet Shard:** for defeating Dark Suicune in Nightmare Petalburg.
- **Seabreeze Shipping HQ Key Card:** found with Luka in the cargo ship hold.
- **Magma Emblem:** from the Mt. Pyre shrine keeper after the Orbs are stolen.
- **Devon Scope:** from Stella on Route 120.
- **Required HMs obtained through story progression:** Flash, Surf, Fly, Dive, and Waterfall. Rock Smash and Strength are obtained from their usual Mauville/Rusturf side stops and are needed for smooth progression.
